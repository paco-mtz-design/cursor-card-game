# Simple Rules to Get Started

## 1. Game Overview

This is a tactical duel card game inspired by turn-based RPG combat.

Two players face off using squads of unique animal-based units with specialized classes and abilities. The game emphasizes fog of war, strategic movement, tactical attacks, and the smart use of items and terrain.

The Tacticlash battlefield evokes the moment when enemy armies are already deployed and facing each other — there's no time for lengthy planning or complex unit positioning. Quick thinking and fast-paced action are required each turn.

---

## 2. Game Objective

**Capture 15** of your opponent's units before they capture 15 of yours.

Players interested in express matches can set **10 captures** as the goal.

---

## 3. Game Components

### Basic Game

- 1 **Unit Deck** (32 unique characters)
- 1 **Item Deck** (62 cards including **Gear**, **Terrain**, and single-use effects)
- **Coin** for coin-flip mechanics
- 2 Battlefields (5×1 slots per player)
- Damage markers (red) and status effect markers (orange)

### Advanced Game (with Seer's Bestiary)

- 1 **Faction Deck** (12 faction cards, with 3 duplicates of each faction)
- 1 **Bestiary Deck** (13 unique Bestiary cards)

- Some units are Rookies, some are Veterans.
- Veteran units have **Veteran Skills** — unique abilities written on their cards.
- Some skills trigger when **Moving**, **Attacking**, being **Hit**, and more.
- Keep an eye on your Veteran units' skills even when they aren't **Attacking** — they can grant important strategic advantages.

## 5. The Items Deck

### Item Types

**Gear**

Equippable cards that enhance a unit's stats or grant new abilities. Only 1 **Gear** card per unit at a time. If the equipped unit is face-down, so is their **Gear**.

- **Armor** — Adds **HP**. Light Armor fits **Lancers**, **Shooters**, and **Casters**. Heavy Armor fits **Brawlers** only.
- **Accessories** — Adds unique passive or active effects. Used to boost mobility, defense, or targeting. Equippable by any class unless otherwise specified on the card.
- **Legendary Weapons (Promotions)** — Class-specific upgrades. Adds +**1 HP** and unlocks a powerful new ability. Each Legendary Weapon is unique (1 copy in the deck).

**Single-Use Items**

- Instant effect, then discard.
- Use for healing, disruption, or tactical tricks.
- Does not count as **Gear**.

**Terrain Cards**

- Placed face-up on any **Tile** to affect the board.
- Grants protection or disrupts movement and attacks.
- Remains on the board until removed.

---

### Stacking Gear

- Stacking is not allowed unless otherwise specified by a card effect: any given unit may have up to one piece of **Gear** equipped at a time.
- You may swap equipped **Gear** to attach new **Gear** — the discarded **Gear** goes immediately to the discard pile.
- A unit that would be captured if their **Gear** is removed (i.e., its **HP** would reach 0) cannot swap **Gear** this way.

---

## 6. Initial Setup

1. Shuffle the **Unit Deck**.
2. Each player draws **5 unit cards** from the unified deck.
3. Each player places these 5 units **face-down**, in the order they desire, on any of their 5 available battlefield slots.
4. Both rows of units face each other symmetrically in a 5×2 grid.
5. Shuffle the **Item Deck** and make room for the discard pile.
6. Start the game.

---

## 7. Turn Structure

### A. Reinforcement Phase

At the start of your turn:

- If any of your units were captured last turn and there are still unit cards in the deck, **draw 1 unit card** per captured unit and **Deploy** it face-down (unless otherwise specified by card effects) in any open slot.
- Draw **1 item card**.

### B. Action Phase

**Use items:**

- Equip any **Gear** as you wish.
- Place any **Terrain** cards as you wish.
- Use or activate any single-use items as you wish.

**Combat:**

- Choose **1 unit** to act.
- You may **Move** 1 slot left or right (**Swap** with an adjacent **Ally** if needed). The **Moved** unit is revealed if face-down.
- You must then **Attack**.
- **Attacking reveals** both the attacker and the **Target**.

*Review the Detailed Rules for a complete breakdown of Combat rules.*

# Detailed Rules

## 1. Fog of War

- Units enter the board face-down, unless otherwise specified by card effects.
- Units are revealed when they **Move**, **Attack**, or are **Hit**.
- Units remain face-down until revealed by game events.

---

## 2. Movement

- Units may **Move** 1 **Tile** left or right per turn, but only if they are selected to **Attack** that turn.
- When **Moving**, units must **Swap Tiles** with any unit occupying the destination **Tile**.
- Units carry **Gear** when they **Move**.
- **Terrain** cards are not carried with movement.
- A **Moved** unit is revealed face-up (swapped **Allies** are not).
- **Regardless of movement, one unit must always Attack each turn.**

---

## 3. Combat

Players must clearly communicate which unit they want to use for **Attacking** each turn by:

- Revealing the attacking unit and any equipped **Gear** (if face-down),
- announcing and executing any **Movement** (optional), then
- declaring the **Target Enemy** unit.

If the **Attack** will leverage a **Veteran Skill** or item card effect, this must be stated before the **Target** is revealed.

Before revealing the **Target**, the **Opponent** must declare any effect that prevents the **Attack** — such as a **Veteran Skill**, a **Terrain** card effect, or a properly positioned **Lancer** attempting to **Counter**. All such events must be resolved before the **Target** is revealed, if the **Attack** remains successful.

Damage is then calculated:

- Base damage is **1 HP**, unless modified by class buffs, **Veteran Skills**, or item effects.

**Lancers**, **Shooters**, and **Casters** have a base of **1 HP**. **Brawlers** have a base of **2 HP**. **HP** can be increased with **Gear** and certain card effects.

- A unit is captured when its **HP** reaches 0.
- Place damage and/or effect markers on surviving units.
- If the **Attack** results in capturing any of the attacking player's units (e.g., from **Counters** or other effects), replace all captured units before passing the turn to the next player.

---

## 4. Endgame

- If the **Unit Deck** is empty, captured units are not replaced.
- If a player has **no units** left on the board, they **lose**.
- If a player cannot **Attack**, they may **Move** and pass.

# Advanced Rules: The Seer's Bestiary

## 1. Initial Setup

1. Shuffle the **Faction Deck** and the **Bestiary Deck**.
2. Reveal 4 random cards from the **Bestiary Deck** and place them in a row near the battlefield.
3. Place 4 random cards from the **Faction Deck** face-down in a row, matching the positions of the 4 face-up Bestiary cards.

---

## 2. Revealing the Fortune

- Every time a player captures 5 units, reveal one of the face-down **Faction** cards (from left to right).
- The effects of the matching **Bestiary** card now apply to the revealed faction for the remainder of the game.
- Repeat when each player hits their 5 and 10 units captured marks.
- The effects of each **Bestiary** card stack and remain valid until the end of the match.
- The same faction may be revealed more than once — this is a valid outcome.

**Express game (10 captures):** Reveal a **Faction** card every time a player captures 4 units (at their 4 and 8 units captured marks).
