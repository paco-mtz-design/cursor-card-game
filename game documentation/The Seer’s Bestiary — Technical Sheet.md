## Seer’s Bestiary Card Deck

Last Update Apr 12, 2026  
When reading the effects of the cards below, consider these rules:

- Each Bestiary card will be revealed paired with a faction card  
- Factions are the following:  
  - Dogs: Howlsworn Creed  
  - Cats: Whisperfang Watch  
  - Birds: Skyward Kin  
  - Reptiles / amphibians: Scalebound Brood  
- The effect in the Bestiary card applies to ALL active units (i.e. in the 5v5 battlefield), from both players  
- So, for instance, if “Primal Alpha” is revealed together with the “Howlsworn Creed” faction card, it means that:  
  - The attacks of ALL Dog type units deal \+1 damage on hit.
